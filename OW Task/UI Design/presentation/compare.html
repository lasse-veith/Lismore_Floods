The Repair Model
Five stages, chained together
Insurance delay
Log-normal, averaging - days. Models the time for insurance to approve a payout before repairs can start.
Repair queue
A Weibull-CDF rollout curve, not linear: a slow start, a steep middle, then a tapering tail as the backlog clears.
Market pricing
A log-normal curve driven by supply and demand. As tradies taking on jobs need more resources, market prices climb quickly, peaking around day -.
Warehouse stockout
Local stock, modelled on - large Lismore warehouses, is used up fast. Once it runs out (day -), a logistics premium applies to materials.
Scope growth
The longer a house sits untouched, the more mold spreads and the more area needs stripping and repainting. This starts at day -, the average time mold takes to establish.
