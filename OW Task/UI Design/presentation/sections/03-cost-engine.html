03 How The Static Cost Engine Was Built

From five real sources to a dollar figure

Every flooded property is priced the instant it first floods above its floor — real addresses and elevation locate and ground it, then nine unit-cost components trigger independently at their own depth threshold, not all-or-nothing.

Schema fields: real vs. synthetic
Full field-by-field breakdown — technical detail

Provenance of every column is traceable — see SYNTHETIC_ASSUMPTIONS.md at the project root for the complete, per-field writeup.

Real / real-derived: property_id, address, suburb, postcode, latitude, longitude, ground_elevation_m_ahd, building_footprint, dwelling_structure_census, peak_water_level_m_ahd.

Synthetic, calibrated: construction type, switchboard type, circuit count, kitchen spec, flooring, building age, every unit cost in base_pricing.py, insurance delay, queue position, job duration, market-pricing surge/stockout parameters, and scope growth.
