## Top pick: live "flood depth cost simulator" widget
The strongest option, because it's not decoration — it **demonstrates the
actual trigger logic** rather than describing it in prose.
 
- A single draggable slider: "depth above floor" (0 to 2m)
- A stylized cross-section of a house (simple side-view illustration),
  with a water-fill line that rises as the slider moves
- Below it, the 9 cost components laid out as a row/grid of cards
  (Flooring, Drying/Decon, Demolition, Kitchen, Appliances, Plasterboard,
  Painting, Switchboard, Electrical) — each card is greyed-out/off until
  its real depth threshold is crossed, then lights up with a real dollar
  figure (using an average/representative property's actual unit costs)
- A running total updates live as the slider moves, so the viewer
  literally watches the depth-tiered logic switch components on at 0m,
  5cm, and 25cm in real time
- This single widget replaces what would otherwise be several paragraphs
  of "here's how the trigger thresholds work" — and it's genuinely
  interactive, not just animated on load
